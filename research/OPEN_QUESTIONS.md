# Open research questions

- What minimum real-user sample supports stable estimates of heterogeneous treatment effects across domains and models?
- Can confidence be calibrated against the probability that a user accepts a preference when shown it?
- Which implicit signals are valid across provider UIs without confusing task-specific editing with stable style preference?
- How much domain transfer is beneficial before it becomes harmful overgeneralization?
- What representation remains expressive while being understandable and directly editable by non-technical users?
- Can preference adherence be measured without rewarding superficial stylistic compliance over factual quality?
- How should the study separate benefits from added instruction tokens, prompt ordering, and model-specific instruction-following ability?
- Does structured personalization reduce privacy exposure in practice, not merely token count?
- Which forms of preference drift are transient session intent versus durable changes?
- How robust is the extractor to prompt injection, quoted text, role-play, and assistant-authored claims?
- What interoperability governance would be required before the profile schema could become a shared standard?
- Which claims require a controlled human study, and which can be established through deterministic system tests?
