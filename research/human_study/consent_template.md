## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to take part in research about whether an editable preference
profile changes how useful AI-generated answers feel across different AI systems.
The system may infer response-format preferences from corrections you make. It is
not intended to infer demographic, health, political, or other sensitive traits.

## What you will do

If you agree, you will:

- select response preferences and rate your familiarity with broad task domains;
- complete supplied, low-stakes prompts and optionally correct answer style;
- rate debranded AI answers and compare pairs of answers;
- inspect and edit a proposed preference profile;
- complete a short questionnaire.

Participation takes about [INSERT] minutes. The AI answers may come from [LIST
PROVIDERS/MODELS]. Do not enter personal, confidential, employer, medical, legal,
or financial information; use only the supplied scenarios.

## Voluntary participation

Taking part is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop without penalty or
loss of benefits. Your decision will not affect your relationship with [INSERT].

## Risks or discomforts

AI answers can be wrong, inappropriate, or frustrating. Preference inferences
can also be incorrect. Although we minimize data, free-text responses can
accidentally contain identifying information, and data sent to an AI provider may
be processed under that provider's terms and our institutional agreement. [INSERT
PROVIDER RETENTION/LOCATION DETAILS AND OTHER REVIEWED RISKS.]

Risk reduction includes synthetic scenarios, warnings against private data,
deidentified study IDs, secure storage, skip/report controls, and the ability to
inspect or delete profile items during the task.

## Benefits

You may receive no direct benefit. The research may help characterize when
portable AI personalization is useful or harmful. We do not promise that the
system will improve your answers or understand you accurately.
