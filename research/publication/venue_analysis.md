# Publication venue analysis

**Facts and availability checked:** 2026-09-12. Dates marked “not announced” were not present in official venue material at the cutoff and must not be inferred from prior years.

## Recommendation

The target depends on the paper that the evidence ultimately supports:

- **Algorithm + benchmark + cross-model experiments:** submit through ACL Rolling Review (ARR) for ACL/EMNLP/NAACL, or use TACL for a mature NLP-centered journal article. The next explicitly listed conference path after the cutoff is the 2026-10-12 ARR cycle for NAACL 2027; ACL 2027's final ARR cycle is listed only as January 2027, day and commitment date not yet announced.
- **User control + transparency + longitudinal user study:** IUI is the best conceptual fit, with CHI appropriate for a substantial HCI contribution. Both IUI 2027 and CHI 2027 paper deadlines had already passed by the cutoff, so no next-cycle date should be promised.
- **Contextual preference learning framed as recommendation/user modeling:** RecSys is strong if the method predicts utility or elicits preferences and includes recommendation-standard offline, online, or user evaluation. RecSys 2027 dates were not announced at the cutoff.
- **Broad AI method:** AAAI is viable only if the algorithmic contribution generalizes beyond language interfaces. AAAI-27 was already closed.
- **Integrated mature system plus human evaluation:** ACM TiiS is plausible as a rolling journal, especially after the work is too complete for a conference page limit.

No venue guarantees publication. The contribution should determine the venue rather than retrofitting one artifact to every community.

## Current ACL/ARR planning facts

The official [ARR dates page](https://aclrollingreview.org/dates) lists ten-week cycles. At the cutoff:

- the 2026-08-03 cycle was in review, with author response scheduled for 2026-09-14–24 and cycle end 2026-10-11;
- EACL 2027 used that August cycle as its final ARR submission, with commitment 2026-10-11;
- the next submission was 2026-10-12, final for NAACL 2027 and COLING 2027, with commitment 2026-12-23;
- ACL 2027 listed a final ARR submission in January 2027 but no exact day or commitment date.

On 2026-09-11, ACL announced a new [sustainable reviewing policy](https://www.aclweb.org/portal/content/acl-sustainable-reviewing-policy) applying from the October 2026 ARR cycle. It ties reviewed capacity to qualified service contributors, adds a lottery for submissions without service capacity, imposes per-author cycle caps, and requires complete OpenReview profiles including ORCID. This is an immediate planning constraint, not a minor administrative detail.

For format and review norms, the official [EMNLP 2026 CFP](https://2026.emnlp.org/calls/main_conference_papers/) is the most recent concrete reference, but its dates are historical after the cutoff. Future ACL-family venue instructions may differ.

## Venue-specific strategy

### ACL / EMNLP / NAACL

Lead with the scientific problem: preference applicability under domain shift and model shift. The strongest version includes a formal profile/selector, reproducible benchmark, several baselines, confidence calibration, negative controls for irrelevant preferences, and multiple model families. The browser extension can be the data-collection and demonstration vehicle, not the central novelty.

ARR permits revision and resubmission after reviews, but submitting to ARR affects TACL eligibility: TACL's official policy imposes a nine-month waiting period from the ARR submission month for papers reviewed there and not accepted. Choose the journal/conference route deliberately.

### CHI

Lead with user agency and the consequences of inferred personalization. Candidate research questions include whether evidence/provenance improves mental models, whether locks and scoped overrides reduce loss of control, and how users perceive local inference of sensitive preferences. A small convenience sample and preference-match metric alone would be weak. The official [CHI 2027 papers page](https://chi2027.acm.org/authors/papers/) requires human-research ethics compliance applicable to the authors' environment and uses revise-and-resubmit.

### IUI

IUI explicitly lists user modeling, personalization, explainability, privacy/security, human control, trust, personalized LLM interaction, and steering. It is unusually well aligned with a paper that contributes both selector/compiler mechanics and an interface study. The official [IUI 2027 CFP](https://iui.acm.org/2027/call-for-papers/) expects evidence appropriate to claims and accepts computational analysis, system evaluation, and user studies.

### RecSys

Frame each preference record as uncertain user state and the selector as a contextual policy optimizing user utility under cost and safety constraints. Compare against contextual-bandit and retrieval baselines, evaluate cold start and temporal drift, and measure beyond accuracy. The official [RecSys 2026 call](https://recsys.acm.org/recsys26/call/) explicitly includes human-centered, explainable, cross-domain, conversational, context-aware, generative, and agentic recommendation.

### AAAI

Use AAAI only if the method is a general contribution to uncertainty-aware user modeling, online learning, or multi-model control. The official [AAAI-27 instructions](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/submission-instructions/) allowed seven non-reference pages and required reproducibility materials at submission, making a sprawling product-plus-study narrative difficult.

### TiiS and TACL

TiiS is appropriate for the mature end-to-end interactive system. The available [TiiS author page](https://media.contaction.li/tiis-2016/for-authors.html) says papers should connect intelligent technology and interaction, but it carries legacy site branding; verify current ScholarOne instructions before submission. Since ACM moved sponsored publications to full OA in 2026, institutional ACM Open coverage or APC/waiver status must be checked for the submission year.

TACL is appropriate if the core contribution remains NLP rather than interface design. Its [submission page](https://transacl.org/ojs/index.php/tacl/about/submissions) lists monthly deadlines and its [about page](https://transacl.org/index.php/tacl/about) states immediate OA with no author charges. It offers space and revision depth but has no ordinary supplementary-material review, so essential experiments must fit the paper.

## Practical publication timeline

Assuming no complete paper existed at the 2026-09-12 cutoff:

1. **September–October 2026:** freeze hypotheses, profile semantics, baselines, and evaluation protocol; complete any required ethics review before recruiting users.
2. **October 2026:** use the 2026-10-12 ARR cycle only if a rigorous manuscript and qualified service contributor are ready; otherwise do not rush merely to reach NAACL 2027.
3. **Late 2026:** run pilot and power checks, then preregister the confirmatory design where appropriate; audit citations again because the field is moving rapidly.
4. **January 2027:** ACL 2027 is a plausible ARR target, but the exact official date/commitment path was TBA at the cutoff and must be rechecked.
5. **Rolling alternative:** TACL for a mature NLP paper that has not triggered its ARR waiting rule; TiiS for the mature combined system/study.
6. **Next HCI/recommender cycles:** monitor official CHI, IUI, and RecSys pages; do not project prior-year dates as announced deadlines.

## Open-access summary

ACL Anthology papers published from 2016 onward are [CC BY 4.0](https://aclanthology.org/faq/copyright/). TACL provides immediate OA without author or submission charges. ACM states that its publications transitioned to full OA from 2026; corresponding authors at ACM Open institutions generally avoid APCs, while others need an APC or qualifying waiver. CHI 2027 explicitly listed $500 member/$750 non-member APCs at the cutoff, but those numbers should not be generalized to other venues or years. AAAI makes proceedings publicly accessible, but licensing and any author charges should be confirmed in the target call.

## Final decision rule

Choose after results, using the paper's primary claim:

- preference-learning or benchmark result → ACL-family / TACL;
- interface, agency, or longitudinal human finding → IUI / CHI / TiiS;
- contextual utility-learning result → RecSys;
- broadly general AI algorithm → AAAI.

The current best default is an **ACL-family empirical paper**, with an **IUI/TiiS path** if the most original evidence is the transparent control experience rather than the learning algorithm.
